01 — About Logan

Performance at scale,
built on attribution.

Logan is an ad tech company with a global footprint across Latin America, Europe, Africa, and the United States, focused on driving measurable business outcomes for brands through commerce and e-commerce media.

The company works with more than 1,200 brands, both directly and through their agencies, including leading players across retail, CPG, and a wide range of other categories. Logan is deeply integrated with the major advertising agency groups in the markets where it operates, positioning itself as a strategic partner rather than a standalone technology vendor.

At its core, Logan is built around performance and attribution. The company helps brands generate and measure results across the full consumer journey, with a strong focus on sales, store visits, and digital interactions across multiple markets. This includes both online and offline environments, enabling a unified view of performance that connects media exposure to real-world outcomes.

In addition to its commercial capabilities, Logan operates as a backbone services provider for global companies, particularly in the area of footfall attribution. Through this role, the company supports large-scale measurement frameworks, delivering campaign attribution, performance analysis, and data-driven insights that inform strategic decision-making.

No DSP. No SSP.

A connective infrastructure layer.

Logan does not operate its own DSP or SSP. Instead, it focuses on integrating and commercializing third-party inventory and technologies, acting as a connective infrastructure layer across the ecosystem. This approach allows the company to remain flexible, partner-driven, and deeply embedded within the evolving media and data landscape, while maintaining a clear focus on efficiency, transparency, and measurable impact.

Full-funnel commerce attribution

From digital impression
to in-store purchase.

  1. Logan Ads delivers the programmatic impression.
  2. Criteo retargets the high-intent shopper across commerce environments.
  3. Cart activity and transaction data are captured.
  4. Logan's Metamobility verifies the in-store visit and purchase.

Logan's Metamobility tracks a user's physical movements and purchase behaviour in the real world. Criteo tracks that same user's online shopping behaviour, cart activity and transactions. Together, we can connect a digital ad impression to an in-store purchase — closing the attribution gap that most advertisers in LATAM still cannot solve.

This means a major CPG advertiser can run a programmatic campaign through Logan Ads, retarget high-intent shoppers via Criteo's commerce engine, and then measure whether those users actually walked into a store and bought the product — using Logan's real-world location data as the attribution layer.

That is a proposition that no other partnership in LATAM can currently offer.

Data monetization & sales lift attribution

A new revenue line
for Criteo.

Beyond the standard sales representation model, the partnership unlocks a new revenue stream for Criteo: data monetization through sales lift measurement and attribution models structured directly with brands. This is a layer that sits on top of media buying and creates incremental margin without overlapping with Criteo's existing operations.

Logan's positioning in commerce media already generates spontaneous demand for this kind of measurement. Brands are actively asking for sales lift attribution — and few players in the region can deliver it at scale. The Criteo × Logan partnership is uniquely positioned to monetize this demand, both in LATAM and in Brazil, without conflicting with Criteo's local operations.

This is a clear competitive differentiator. Aleph cannot bring this to the table — they are positioned as a sales representation layer, not as a data and attribution layer. Logan can. Adding this revenue line to the standard sales rep model is what makes the proposition structurally superior.
